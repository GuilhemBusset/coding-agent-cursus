Session 1 · Sources

Sources, act by act

Every source the decks, labs and exercise pages of Session 1 cite, in the order of the day, with the date each one was last read.

How to read this list

The fact-check pass of 2026-10-18 re-read every source below against the claim it supports, one week before the session. Models, prices and CLI flags change month to month: the as-of date next to a claim on a slide is the last day it was checked.

  • Sources are primary unless marked: the vendor’s own documentation, the paper, the lab’s or company’s own post. A secondary source is marked, and no claim rests on one alone.
  • Some sites refuse automated reading (openai.com, openreview.net, doi.org, articles on x.com). Their entries say how they were read instead.
  • Repository documents are named by path. Install scripts that the pre-work pipes to a shell are not listed: their documentation pages are.

What the pass changed, claim by claim, is in the fact-check log.
