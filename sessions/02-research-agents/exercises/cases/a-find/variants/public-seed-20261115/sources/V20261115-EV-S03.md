## V20261115-EV-S03:P01

Harbor Repair (harbor-repair) fixes candidate truck routes for a repair pass, then allocates charging against a per-depot, per-type, per-slot occupancy ledger. A slot is usable only while its count is below the finite charger count. Conflicts move to another valid waiting slot or trigger leg cancellation and route rebuilding; an unresolved conflict is never returned as feasible.

## V20261115-EV-S03:P02

Harbor Repair assumes the same known deterministic inputs and 30-minute whole-wait, lossless constant-power charging as V20261115-EV-S01:P02. It checks coverage, deadheads and return home, truck and charger power, maintenance, battery bounds and final state of charge after repair or cancellation. Fixed candidate routes and repair-or-cancel make this a feasible-plan heuristic, with no claim of global cost optimality.
