## V20261125-EV-S02:P01

Cedar Ledger (cedar-ledger) is a mixed-integer daily-cost model. Binary truck-leg assignments and cancellations, deadhead links and charge-slot choices enforce coverage and the truck day. For each depot, charger type and slot, the sum of simultaneous charging trucks cannot exceed the available charger count. It therefore represents limited shared capacity, including separate slow and fast pools.

## V20261125-EV-S02:P02

Cedar Ledger uses the deterministic 30-minute, whole-wait, lossless constant-power assumptions in V20261125-EV-S01:P02. It enforces truck and charger power bounds, maintenance exclusion, intermediate battery bounds and final home state of charge at least the initial level. It may optimise cost, but a time-limited feasible solution is not an optimality certificate.
