## EV-S04:P01

Slot Ledger Private (private-ledger) gives every truck its own always-available charger. It checks individual energy and charging power but has no depot occupancy sum. It does not model a finite shared charger pool; calling it Slot Ledger without the Private suffix confuses two different formulations.
